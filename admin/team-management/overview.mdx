This section covers all aspects of administering your Checkly account, from managing team members and permissions to setting up advanced security features and API access.

## Team Member Roles

Checkly has five built-in roles: **Owner**, **Admin**, **Read & Write**, **Read & Run**, and **Read Only**. Each role inherits all permissions from the roles below it. If [custom roles](/admin/team-management/custom-roles) (beta) are enabled for your account, you can also define roles with the permissions you pick.

Each account has one Owner. The account creator is the Owner until they [transfer ownership](#transferring-ownership). You can invite teammates with any other role. You can [change a member's role](/admin/team-management/managing-team-members#change-members’-roles) after they join.

### Permissions and roles

Your account role grants permissions for actions on resources. Use the [permissions reference](/admin/team-management/permissions) to find each permission’s ID, name in the role editor, description, and how to request access.

### Choosing the Right Role

| Role | Best for |
|------|----------|
| **Owner** | Account creator with full control over billing and ownership |
| **Admin** | Team leads who manage members, settings, and infrastructure |
| **Read & Write** | Developers who create and maintain checks |
| **Read & Run** | QA engineers or CI/CD pipelines that run tests but shouldn't modify configuration |
| **Read Only** | Stakeholders who need visibility into monitoring status |

### Transferring Ownership

**Owners** can transfer ownership to another team member from the [General](https://app.checklyhq.com/settings/account/general) section in account settings:

1. Make sure the new Owner is a member of the account. If they aren't yet, [invite them](/admin/team-management/adding-team-members) and wait until they accept. Pending invitations can't receive ownership.
2. In the **Transfer ownership** section, click "Transfer ownership".
3. Pick the new Owner by their email address and click "Transfer ownership".

The new Owner gets the role right away. They don't need to accept the transfer. Your role will change to **Admin** after the transfer.

- Only the Owner can transfer ownership. Admins can't make themselves the Owner.
- The button is disabled while you're the only member of the account.
- If your account is managed through the [Vercel native integration](/integrations/ci-cd/vercel/managing-plan#user-management), transfer ownership in your integration settings on Vercel.
- The [account name](#account-name) doesn't change with the transfer.

After the transfer, you can [leave the account](/admin/team-management/managing-team-members#leave-an-account), or the new Owner can [remove you](/admin/team-management/managing-team-members#remove-members). User API keys stop working for the account when their user leaves it. Use [service API keys](/admin/creating-api-key) for automation that shouldn't depend on one member.

To move ownership to another email address, for example a shared team mailbox, invite that address, transfer ownership to it, and then remove your old user. See [Changing your email](/admin/changing-your-email-password#changing-your-email).

If the current Owner is no longer at your company or can no longer access Checkly, reach out to [support@checklyhq.com](mailto:support@checklyhq.com) and we can transfer ownership for you. To verify that you're authorized to request the change, we'll ask you to provide a previous Checkly receipt or invoice.

## Account Management

### Account name

The account Owner can change the account name from the [General](https://app.checklyhq.com/settings/account/general) section in account settings. By default the account name is the Owner's email address, but you can change it to reflect a department, product team, etc.
