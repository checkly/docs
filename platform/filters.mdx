On **Monitors** and **Heartbeats**, you filter the list in the filter field above it. Each filter is a block with a field, an operator and a value, such as **status is failing**. Filters join with **and** or **or**. Groups let you mix both.

## Add a filter

Click the filter field to see every field you can filter on, each with a short description. Pick one, or type the start of its name and press Enter.

You can also type a whole filter as `field:operator:value` and press Enter, for example `status:is:failing` or `availability:<:99.9`. Put values with spaces in quotes: `name:contains:'checkout api'`.

Text that isn't a filter searches by name. Type it and press Enter.

Fields with a fixed set of values, such as **Status** or **Tags**, open a picker. Fields like **Tags** take several values. The filter then matches any of them. The block then shows how many it holds, such as **3 tags**. Long lists, such as your tags, have a search at the top of the picker.

## Fields

| Screen | Fields |
|--------|--------|
| Monitors | Name, Group, URL, Logical ID, Status, Check type, Tags, Project, Traces and the time range |
| Heartbeats | Name, Ping URL, Status, Tags, Period, Grace, Availability and the time range |

- **Text fields** such as **Name**, **Group** and **URL** match when the text holds every word you type, in any order.
- **Tags** on Monitors match the tags of a check and the tags of its group.
- **Project** shows when the account has checks deployed with the [Checkly CLI](/cli/overview). **Traces** shows when the account [imports traces](/resolve/traces/import/overview).
- **Period** and **Grace** compare durations such as `30s`, `5m`, `1h` or `1d`. **Availability** compares the percentage for the selected time range. These three take `>`, `>=`, `<`, `<=` and `=`.
- **The time range** is always set. You can change it but not remove it. It always applies with **and**.

## Join filters with and or or

Filters join with **and** by default. The list then shows what matches every filter. Click the word between two filters to switch it to **or**. The list then shows what matches any filter. The switch applies to every join at that level.

To join only the next filter with **or**, type `or` before it. With **status is failing and name contains api** in the field, typing `or tags:is:critical` and pressing Enter gives **status is failing and (name contains api or tags is critical)**. The new filter is grouped with the filter right before it. The other joins don't change. Typing `and` before a filter works the same way.

## Group filters

A group holds filters joined by its own **and** or **or**. Groups let one query mix both. Click the word inside a group to switch it.

To build a group as you type, type `(` in the filter field. An empty group opens with the cursor inside it. The filters you add next go into it. Type `or` before a filter to join the group's filters with **or**. Type `)` to close the group and go back to the top level. Leaving the field or pressing Escape also closes it.

For example, typing `status:is:failing`, then `(`, `tags:is:critical`, `or name:contains:checkout` and `)`, pressing Enter after each filter, gives **status is failing and (tags is critical or name contains checkout)**.

With the mouse:

- **Make a group:** drop a filter onto another one.
- **Move filters:** drag a filter to reorder it, or to move it into or out of a group.
- **Move a group:** drag it by its handle.

Groups hold filters only. A group can't hold another group.

For example, on Heartbeats, `status:is:failing and (period:>:1h or availability:<:99.9)` finds failing heartbeats that either expect a ping less often than hourly or are below 99.9% available. On Monitors, `status:is:failing or tags:is:critical` lists every failing check and every check tagged `critical`, whatever its status.

## Use the keyboard

Every part of a filter is reachable from the keyboard.

| Key | What it does |
|-----|--------------|
| Tab, Shift+Tab, Left, Right | Move between the parts of the filters and the joining words |
| Typing on a value | Changes the value, or searches the picker of a field with many values |
| Enter, Space | Opens the picker of the focused part, or switches the focused joining word |
| Backspace, Delete | Removes the focused filter |
| Alt+Left, Alt+Right | Moves the focused filter, into and out of groups too |

In the filter field, type `and` or `or` and press Enter to switch how the filters at the top level join. Inside an open group, it switches the group's join instead.

## Save and share filters

The page URL holds every filter and its groups. Copy it to send the same list to a teammate. To keep filters you use often one click away, save them as a [view](/platform/views).
