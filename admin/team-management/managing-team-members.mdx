**Account settings > Members** lists the members of your account, and under the invite form, the invitations that haven't been accepted yet. From there you change members' roles, remove members, resend or remove invitations, change your own role, or leave the account. To invite someone, see [Adding team members](/admin/team-management/adding-team-members).

## The members list

The **Account members** table shows each member's **Name**, **Email**, **SSO** and **MFA** status, **Role** and **Created** date. Invitations are listed separately, in [Pending invites](#pending-invites).

When the account has more than one member, these filters sit above the table:

- The search field matches names and emails.
- **Role** keeps the members holding the roles you pick.
- **Has SSO** keeps members who signed in through single sign-on.
- **Has MFA** keeps members with multi-factor authentication turned on.
- **Editable by you** keeps the members you can act on, the ones with a checkbox. It only shows when you can act on someone.

## Select members

A row has a checkbox when you can act on it: a member whose role you can change or whom you can remove. Your own row and the owner's never have one.

- The checkbox in the table header selects every row you can act on.
- On a phone, each card has its own checkbox.
- A row that a filter hides leaves the selection, so an action only touches rows you can see.

With rows checked, a bar at the bottom of the screen shows how many are selected and offers:

- **Select all**, with the number of rows you can act on, while some of them aren't checked.
- **Change role** and **Remove**. An action that can't apply to the selection is disabled, and hovering it tells you why.
- The **x** button, which clears the selection. Pressing Escape while the bar has focus does the same.

On a phone, the bar is docked to the bottom edge, and its actions open from **Actions**.

## Change members' roles

1. Select the members and click **Change role**.
2. Pick a role. The picker lists the built-in roles, then any [custom roles](/admin/team-management/custom-roles) (beta). Each role shows its description under its name. You can type to search. The picker only offers roles you can give to every member you selected.
3. Click **Change role**.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/team-management/members-change-role.png" alt="The Change role dialog with the role picker open, listing each role with its description below a search field" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/team-management/members-change-role-dark.png" alt="The Change role dialog with the role picker open, listing each role with its description below a search field" />
</Frame>

Members lose any permission the new role doesn't hold. These rules apply:

- You can only change the role of members with less access than you, and only to a role whose permissions you hold yourself.
- The owner's role only changes by [transferring ownership](/admin/team-management/overview#transferring-ownership).
- Invitations keep the role they were sent with. To invite someone with another role, remove their invitation and invite them again.
- On the Hobby plan, changing roles needs an upgrade to the Team plan.
- If the change isn't allowed for one of the members when you confirm, for example because their role changed in the meantime, nobody's role changes and an error tells you why.
- Members who left the account after you loaded the page are skipped. The others get the new role, and a message names the ones that were skipped.

When the role you pick holds exactly your own permissions, as when an Admin makes a member an Admin, the dialog warns you. Those members become your peers: you can no longer change their role or remove them. Only someone with more access than you, such as the owner, can undo it.

## Remove members

Select the members and click **Remove**. The confirmation names the member when you remove one, and counts them when you remove several, as in **Remove 2 members?**. If any of them are in an on-call rotation, it links the schedules and warns that their shifts become unassigned.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/team-management/members-remove.png" alt="Confirmation dialog titled Remove 2 members?, saying the members lose access right away" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/team-management/members-remove-dark.png" alt="Confirmation dialog titled Remove 2 members?, saying the members lose access right away" />
</Frame>

- Removed members lose access to the account right away.
- You can only remove members with less access than you. The owner can't be removed.
- If a member you selected left the account after you loaded the page, Checkly removes the others and tells you who was skipped.

<Note>
A removed member who signs in through the account's SSO connection can come back. If they aren't a member of any other Checkly account, they rejoin the next time they open Checkly, with the **Default Role** set in **Account settings > SSO configuration**, as long as [JIT provisioning](/admin/team-management/single-sign-on#just-in-time-jit-provisioning) is on and the account has room for another member. To keep them out, remove their access in your identity provider, or have the owner turn off **JIT Provisioning** on that page.
</Note>

## Pending invites

Under the invite form, **Pending invites** lists the invitations that haven't been accepted yet, with their **Email** and **Role**. It shows when there are pending invitations and you can invite members, with their number next to the title. Click the title to fold or unfold the list. When the account reaches its plan's member limit, the invite form goes away but the list stays, so you can remove invitations to free seats.

  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/team-management/pending-invites-dark.png" alt="The Invite new members card with the invite form and, below it, the Pending invites list with a count of 2, showing each invitation's email, role, a Resend button and a remove button" />
</Frame>

- **Resend** sends the invitation email again, and the invitation stays valid for another month.
- The trash button removes the invitation after you confirm. A removed invitation can no longer be accepted.
- You can resend or remove any invitation, whoever sent it.
- If the invitation was accepted or removed after you loaded the page, nothing is removed and a message tells you which happened. An accepted invitation isn't removed, because the person is now a member and shows in the members list.

## Change your own role

Click **Change your role** above the members table, next to **Leave account**. Pick a role and click **Change your role**.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/team-management/members-change-own-role.png" alt="The Change your role dialog with your current role in the role picker and a note that only the account owner or an admin can give back the permissions you lose" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/team-management/members-change-own-role-dark.png" alt="The Change your role dialog with your current role in the role picker and a note that only the account owner or an admin can give back the permissions you lose" />
</Frame>

- You can only move to a role whose permissions you already hold, so changing your own role never adds to your access.
- You need the **Lower your own role** permission. Without it, or without a role you can move to, the button doesn't show.
- You lose any permission the new role doesn't hold. To get them back, ask the owner or an admin.
- The owner changes role only by [transferring ownership](/admin/team-management/overview#transferring-ownership).
- The button is disabled on the Hobby plan and on accounts managed through Vercel.

On a phone, **Change your role** and **Leave account** are in the **Your membership** menu next to the table's title.

## Leave an account

Click **Leave account** above the members table and confirm. You lose access to the account right away, and Checkly switches you to another of your accounts, or signs you out if you have none. To come back, ask the owner or an admin to invite you again.

If you're in an on-call rotation, the confirmation links the schedules and warns that your shifts become unassigned, so you can arrange a replacement first.

You can't leave an account:

- **As the owner.** [Transfer ownership](/admin/team-management/overview#transferring-ownership) first.
- **When your SSO connection signs you into it.** Your access comes from the connection.
- **When it's managed through Vercel.** Its members are managed in Vercel.

## Permissions

Every role can see the members list. Acting on it needs these permissions:

| Permission | What it allows | Built-in roles |
|------------|----------------|----------------|
| Change members' roles | Give members with less access than you a role whose permissions you hold | Owner, Admin |
| Remove members | Remove members with less access than you | Owner, Admin |
| Invite members | Invite people, and resend or remove any invitation | Owner, Admin |
| Lower your own role | Move yourself to a role whose permissions you already hold | Owner, Admin, Read & Write |

The owner holds **Lower your own role**, but changes role only by transferring ownership. [Custom roles](/admin/team-management/custom-roles) (beta) can grant these permissions one by one. See [Team member roles](/admin/team-management/overview#team-member-roles) for what each role can do.
