<Note>
Custom roles are in beta and are not generally available. This documentation applies only to accounts where custom roles are enabled.
</Note>

A custom role is a named set of permissions that you pick one by one. Custom roles sit next to the [built-in roles](/admin/team-management/overview#team-member-roles). You can assign them to members, invitations, and [service API keys](/admin/creating-api-key#service-api-keys).

## The Roles page

**Account settings > Roles** lists your account's custom roles. It shows once custom roles are turned on for your account. Each row has the role's name and description, how many permissions it grants, and how many members and pending invitations hold it. Click a role to open its page.

| Permission | What it allows | Built-in roles |
|------------|----------------|----------------|
| View roles | See the Roles page and open a role to see its permissions | All roles |
| Create roles | Create custom roles | Owner, Admin |
| Edit roles | Change a role's name, description and permissions | Owner, Admin |
| Delete roles | Delete custom roles and choose a replacement for affected holders | Owner, Admin |

A custom role can hold these permissions too. They're listed under **Roles**.

A role's page shows its description, the permissions it grants, and who holds it.

- The permissions are grouped by area, with a count next to each area. Expand an area to see its permissions.
- **Members** lists the members who hold the role. It also lists the service API keys that hold it when you can see API keys. Pending invitations are counted below the table.
- **Edit role** opens the role in the editor. It shows when you have **Edit roles**.
- **Delete role** shows when you have **Delete roles**.

## Create a role

1. On the Roles page, click **New role**.
2. Enter a **Name** and, if you like, a **Description**. The description shows in the role picker.
3. Add the permissions the role grants, as [Add and remove permissions](#add-and-remove-permissions) describes. To start from an existing role, click **Copy permissions from…**, pick the role, and click **Copy permissions**. You can copy any built-in role other than Owner, or another custom role. Copying replaces the role's permissions. It leaves out the permissions you can't give. A message lists them.
4. Click **Create role**. The role's page opens.

- Names can be up to 100 characters.
- A name must differ from every other role's name in the account, ignoring case.
- A name can't be the name of a built-in role.
- An account can have up to 50 custom roles.

## Add and remove permissions

The editor uses permission names such as **Run checks**. If another guide refers to an ID such as `checks:run`, see [Permission names and IDs](/admin/team-management/permissions) to find its name, resource, and how to get access.

You add permissions from the **Add a permission, a resource or a whole area** field.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/team-management/custom-roles-add-permissions.png" alt="The permission field with edit checks typed in. Edit checks is selected, and the preview says it also adds 7 permissions it needs, with an Add 8 permissions button" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/team-management/custom-roles-add-permissions-dark.png" alt="The permission field with edit checks typed in. Edit checks is selected, and the preview says it also adds 7 permissions it needs, with an Add 8 permissions button" />
</Frame>

- Click the field to browse. **All permissions** comes first, then every area, then each area's resources and permissions. Type to filter them by name or description.
- The preview next to the list describes the selected entry. It lists the permissions the entry needs. Those come along when you add it. Adding **Edit checks**, for example, also adds **View checks** and the other permissions the check editor needs.
- Press Enter or click the **Add** button to add the entry.
- **All permissions** gives the role every permission you can give. Its description says how that compares with the Admin role.
- You can only give permissions you hold yourself. The others aren't offered.

Below the field, the role's permissions are grouped by area, then by resource.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/team-management/custom-roles-editor.png" alt="The permissions of the On-call responder role, grouped by area and then by resource, each permission with its description and a remove button" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/team-management/custom-roles-editor-dark.png" alt="The permissions of the On-call responder role, grouped by area and then by resource, each permission with its description and a remove button" />
</Frame>

- Each area and resource shows how many of its permissions the role holds. It shows **All permissions** when the role holds every one of them.
- A permission that others need names them.
- To remove permissions, click the **×** next to a permission or a resource, or **Remove area** for a whole area. Removing a permission also removes the permissions that need it. The permissions it needed stay.
- Areas and resources fold from their headings.
- Every role can view the account, its members and maintenance windows. The editor doesn't list those permissions.
- Deleting the account and transferring ownership stay with the owner. No custom role can hold them.

## Edit a role

Open the role and click **Edit role**. Make your changes and click **Save role**. Changes apply right away to the members, pending invitations, and service API keys that hold the role.

- An area you changed shows a dot and a **Revert** button. **Revert** puts that area back the way the role was saved.
- When you scroll, **Save role** stays at the top of the page. With unsaved changes, **Discard** shows next to it and undoes all of them.
- You can only edit a role when you hold all of its permissions yourself.

## Delete a role

Click the trash button in the role's row, or **Delete role** on the role's page. If the role is assigned, review the affected members, pending invitations, and service API keys, then choose their **Replacement role** before confirming. Their permissions change immediately. Deleting a role can't be undone.

You must be allowed to assign the replacement to every affected holder. If no suitable replacement is available to you, ask your account owner or an admin to make the change.

## Give a custom role

You pick custom roles wherever you pick a role: when you [invite people](/admin/team-management/adding-team-members), [change members' roles](/admin/team-management/managing-team-members#change-members-roles) or [change your own role](/admin/team-management/managing-team-members#change-your-own-role). The picker lists the built-in roles first, then the custom roles by name.

  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/team-management/custom-roles-picker.png" alt="The Change role dialog with the role picker open, listing the built-in roles and then the custom roles Billing manager and On-call responder" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/team-management/custom-roles-picker-dark.png" alt="The Change role dialog with the role picker open, listing the built-in roles and then the custom roles Billing manager and On-call responder" />

The same rules apply as for built-in roles:

- You can only give a role whose permissions you hold yourself.
- You can only give it to members with less access than you. A member has less access than you when you hold every permission of their role and at least one more.
- You can only move yourself to a role whose permissions you already hold.
- To give custom roles, your own role needs **View roles**. Every built-in role has it.

## API keys

[User API keys](/admin/creating-api-key#user-api-keys) act with their member's role. A member with a custom role has the same permissions through the API and the CLI. Changes to the role apply to their keys right away. When the beta is enabled, [service API keys](/admin/creating-api-key#service-api-keys) can also use a custom role whose permissions you hold.
