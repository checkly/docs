Checkly publishes its security, privacy and legal information on its website. This page points you to the right place for each topic.

## Security and certifications

Checkly is SOC 2 Type 2 compliant and GDPR compliant. The FAQ on the [Checkly security page](https://www.checklyhq.com/security/) covers what data Checkly stores, encryption, data retention, and where your data is stored.

## Requesting compliance documents

Checkly shares the following documents on request:

- The SOC 2 Type 2 report
- A bridge letter that covers the time since the last report period ended
- A penetration test summary

These documents aren't available in the Checkly app or for public download. To request them, email [sales@checklyhq.com](mailto:sales@checklyhq.com).

If your account is on a paid plan, you don't need a non-disclosure agreement (NDA), because section **4. Confidentiality** of the [Terms of Use](https://www.checklyhq.com/terms/) covers the documents. If you're not on a paid plan or not yet a customer, you sign an NDA with Checkly before you receive them.

### Security questionnaires and vendor assessments

Filling in security questionnaires is part of the **Security Review** included in the Enterprise plan, as listed on the [pricing page](https://www.checklyhq.com/pricing/). If you're an Enterprise customer, send your questionnaire to your account executive. On other plans, Checkly doesn't fill in security questionnaires, but you can request the documents above on any plan.

## Data Processing Addendum

If you're established in the EEA, the UK or California, or you provide Checkly with personal data of individuals located there, the Data Processing Addendum is part of the Checkly Terms of Use. See section 5.2, under **5. Compliance**, in the [Terms of Use](https://www.checklyhq.com/terms/).

## Privacy policy

The [Privacy Policy](https://www.checklyhq.com/privacy/) describes how Checkly collects, uses and discloses your information, which third-party providers it uses, and how long check results are retained on each plan.

## Reporting a security issue

The [Security Response page](https://www.checklyhq.com/security-response/) describes how to report a vulnerability. Send urgent reports to [security@checklyhq.com](mailto:security@checklyhq.com). Checkly acknowledges your report, investigates the issue, and credits you for the discovery once the issue is resolved.

## Deleting your account and data

If you're the account owner, you can delete your Checkly account yourself: open **Account settings** > **General**, and in the **Danger zone** section click **Delete my account**. Deleting your account is permanent and removes all checks, data and account history. See [Deleting your Checkly account](/admin/deleting-your-account) for the full steps and requirements.

To have Checkly delete your personal information, email [support@checklyhq.com](mailto:support@checklyhq.com), as described in the [Privacy Policy](https://www.checklyhq.com/privacy/).
