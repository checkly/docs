Your user settings change how Checkly looks and works for you. They don't change anything for your teammates. Checkly saves them to your user. They apply in every account you're a member of and on every device you sign in from.

To open them, click your name at the bottom of the left sidebar and select **User settings**. You can also open **Account settings** from the left sidebar and select **User settings** at the bottom of its menu.

**Profile**, **Appearance**, **Accessibility** and **Time and region** show a preview next to their settings. On these pages, changes apply as soon as you make them. Only **Full name** needs **Save**.

## Profile

[**Profile**](https://app.checklyhq.com/settings/user/profile) sets the name your teammates see in member lists, on-call schedules and incident updates. Enter your name in **Full name** and click **Save**. The **How teammates see you** preview shows the result.

Checkly shows the picture from your sign-in provider. You can't change it in Checkly.

## Appearance

[**Appearance**](https://app.checklyhq.com/settings/user/appearance) sets how Checkly looks to you.

### Theme

Pick **Light**, **Dark** or **Auto**. **Auto** follows the light or dark setting of your operating system.

### Increase contrast

Turn on **Increase contrast** for stronger text, borders and focus rings. It works with the light and the dark theme.

### Status colors

Checkly colors check results, charts and badges by status. Pick the palette whose colors you tell apart best:

- **Standard** uses green, yellow and red.
- **Protanopia and deuteranopia** is for red-green color blindness.
- **Tritanopia** is for blue-yellow color blindness.

### Table density

Pick **Normal** or **Dense**. **Normal** gives each table row more room. **Dense** shortens the rows to fit more of a table on screen, for example on the **Monitors** page.

## Accessibility

[**Accessibility**](https://app.checklyhq.com/settings/user/accessibility) has settings for motion, keyboard shortcuts and notifications. **Increase contrast** is on the [Appearance](#increase-contrast) page, next to the theme.

- **Reduce motion and animations** turns off animations such as sliding panels, chart animations and smooth scrolling. Checkly also reduces motion when your operating system is set to reduce motion.
- **Keyboard shortcuts** are on by default. Turn them off if they clash with your screen reader or browser extensions.
- **Notifications timeout** sets how long notifications stay on screen before they close: 5, 10, 15 or 20 seconds. The default is 10 seconds.

## Time and region

[**Time and region**](https://app.checklyhq.com/settings/user/time-and-region) sets how Checkly shows dates and times. Check results, charts and alert timelines use these settings.

- **Time zone** is your browser's time zone by default. Pick another time zone, or pick **UTC** to match the timestamps in logs and other tools.
- **Time format** is **24-hour** by default. Switch to **12-hour** to show times with AM and PM.
- **Week starts on** is **Sunday** by default. Switch to **Monday** to start the week on Monday in date pickers.

## Folding sidebar sections

You can fold any section of the left sidebar, such as **Detect** or **Communicate**, by clicking its name. Click the name again to unfold it. This works in the main menu and in the **Account settings** menu. Each browser remembers the sections you folded in it. Folded sections aren't saved to your user.

## Other user settings

User settings also has these pages:

- **Security**, where you [change your email or password](/admin/changing-your-email-password) and set up [multi-factor authentication](/admin/team-management/multi-factor-authentication).
- **API keys**, where you create [user API keys](/admin/creating-api-key#user-api-keys).
- **On-call notifications**, where you add your contact methods and set the rules for how Checkly reaches you when you're paged.
