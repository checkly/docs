Checkly publishes its security, privacy and legal information on its website. This page points you to the right place for each topic.

## Security and certifications

The [Checkly security page](https://www.checklyhq.com/security/) states that Checkly is SOC 2 Type 2 compliant and GDPR compliant. Its FAQ also answers common questions about what data Checkly stores, encryption, data retention and where your data is stored.

## Data Processing Addendum

Section 5.2 of the [Terms of Use](https://www.checklyhq.com/terms/) covers the Data Processing Addendum. If you're established in the EEA, the UK or California, or you provide Checkly with personal data of individuals located there, the Data Processing Addendum is incorporated into the Terms. The Terms give its location as [checklyhq.com/privacy](https://www.checklyhq.com/privacy/).

## Privacy policy

The [Privacy Policy](https://www.checklyhq.com/privacy/) describes how Checkly collects, uses and discloses your information, which third-party providers it uses, and how long check results are retained on each plan.

## Reporting a security issue

The [Security Response page](https://www.checklyhq.com/security-response/) describes how to report a vulnerability. Send urgent reports to [security@checklyhq.com](mailto:security@checklyhq.com). Checkly acknowledges your report, investigates the issue, and credits you for the discovery once the issue is resolved.

## Deleting your account and data

If you're the account owner, you can delete your Checkly account from your account settings. See [Deleting your Checkly account](/admin/deleting-your-account).

The [security page](https://www.checklyhq.com/security/) states that Checkly deletes accounts upon request. Under the [Privacy Policy](https://www.checklyhq.com/privacy/), you can request deletion of your personal information by emailing [support@checklyhq.com](mailto:support@checklyhq.com).
