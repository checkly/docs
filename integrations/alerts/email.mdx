## Basic Email Configuration

To receive alerts, you'll need to add one or more valid email addresses, choose what states you want to receive alerts for, and then also add subscriptions to checks.

1. Log in to Checkly and navigate to [Alert Settings](https://app.checklyhq.com/alerts/settings).
2. Click **Add more channels**, find **Email** on the list, and click **Add channel**.
3. Enter the email address. You can use an individual address or a distribution list.
4. Select the alert types you want to receive: failure, degraded, and recovery.
5. Subscribe the checks, monitors, or check groups that should notify this address, then save the channel.

After you create the channel, it appears in the **Alert Channels** section of each check's and monitor's alert settings. You can turn it on or off per check there.

<Note>
Creating alert channels requires the **Manage alert settings** permission. The Owner, Admin, and Read & Write roles have it. Read & Run and Read Only users can't add email addresses. See [Team member roles](/admin/team-management/overview#team-member-roles).
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

### "Inactive addresses" error

If you see this error, this means that your email address has been added to our email suppression list:

> You tried to send to recipient(s) that have been marked as inactive. Found inactive addresses: `name@example.com`. Inactive recipients are ones that have generated a hard bounce, a spam complaint, or a manual suppression.

If we send an email to your email address and your email provider rejects it with a hard bounce, then we automatically add that recipient address to our email suppression list. This ensures that we don't send emails that permanently can't be delivered, like to deleted email addresses. However, sometimes transient issues with your email provider can also return a hard bounce.

If you see this error or if you're not recieving emails from Checkly, reach out to [support@checklyhq.com](mailto:support@checklyhq.com). We can check if your email is on our suppression list and re-activate it.

If the same address keeps getting suppressed after re-activation, please check with your IT or email administrator to understand why your email provider keeps hard bouncing emails from Checkly.
