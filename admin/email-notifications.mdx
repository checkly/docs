Checkly sends a few kinds of email. Some you control yourself, others are part of running your account.

## Alert emails

Alerts about your checks are sent through [alert channels](/communicate/alerts/channels). To change who gets alert emails, or to stop them, edit the email alert channel or which checks are subscribed to it.

## Weekly summary

Checkly sends a weekly summary email about the checks in your account. To turn it off:

1. Open **Settings** and, under **Account**, select [**Preferences**](https://app.checklyhq.com/settings/account/my-preferences).
2. Under **Notifications**, clear **Yes, send me the weekly summary email**.

The setting applies to the account you have selected. If you're a member of several accounts, change it in each of them.

## Account and billing emails

Some emails are part of running your account and can't be turned off, for example invitations, usage and quota warnings, payment problems and plan changes. Usage, billing and payment emails go to the account's Owners and Admins.

## Checkly platform status

Checkly's own [status page](https://is.checkly.online/) shows incidents and maintenance affecting Checkly. Subscribe there to get updates by email.
