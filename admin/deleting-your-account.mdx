You can delete your Checkly account from your account settings:

1. Open **Account settings** from the left sidebar, then select [**General**](https://app.checklyhq.com/settings/account/general). 
2. At the bottom of the page, you'll find a **Danger zone** section where you can click **Delete my account**.
3. Type your account name to confirm the deletion.

<Warning>
Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All checks, data, and account history will be removed.
</Warning>

You must be the [account owner](/admin/team-management/overview/#team-member-roles) to delete your account, and the account must be on the Hobby plan or a trial. If you're on a paid plan, [cancel your subscription](/admin/billing#cancelling) first. Your account moves to the Hobby plan at the end of the billing period, and you can delete it from then on.

If your account is managed through the Vercel Marketplace, delete it from your integration settings on Vercel.

If you need help, reach out to [support@checklyhq.com](mailto:support@checklyhq.com).
